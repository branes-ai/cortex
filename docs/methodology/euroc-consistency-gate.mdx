For each sequence, track-noise level S, seed and R (matched to S, or the filter's default): fit a smooth truth through the ground truth, synthesize the IMU and the feature tracks from it with known noise, run the backend under test, score its trajectory and pose consistency against that truth; then judge the grid. Every backend runs with the same S6d gate.

## Sources

- **kim1995** — M.-J. Kim, M.-S. Kim and S. Y. Shin, "A General Construction Scheme for Unit Quaternion Curves with Simple High Order Derivatives", SIGGRAPH 1995 (the cumulative B-spline on rotations).
- **sommer2020** — C. Sommer, V. Usenko, D. Schubert, N. Demmel and D. Cremers, "Efficient Derivative Computation for Cumulative B-Splines on Lie Groups", CVPR 2020 (angular velocity and acceleration of a cumulative spline).
- **mueggler2018** — E. Mueggler, G. Gallego, H. Rebecq and D. Scaramuzza, "Continuous-Time Visual-Inertial Odometry for Event Cameras", IEEE T-RO 34(6), 2018 (an IMU synthesized from a spline trajectory).
- **barshalom2001** — Y. Bar-Shalom, X. R. Li and T. Kirubarajan, "Estimation with Applications to Tracking and Navigation", Wiley 2001 (NEES, NIS and their χ² bands).
- **zhang2018** — Z. Zhang and D. Scaramuzza, "A Tutorial on Quantitative Trajectory Evaluation for Visual(-Inertial) Odometry", IROS 2018 (4-DoF alignment, ATE, RPE).

### Contracts

**fit_spline** (G1, Fit the truth)

- *Pre:* the ground truth covers the sequence; no gap longer than one knot interval
- *Post:* pose, ω and a are defined on the span; ω and a agree with finite differences of the pose; the deviation from the ground truth is reported

**synth_imu** (G2, Synthesize the IMU)

- *Pre:* the IMU timestamps lie inside the spline's span
- *Post:* noise-free, the shipped S2 integrates it back to the spline's pose; the noise statistics match the densities

**synth_tracks** (G3, Synthesize the tracks)

- *Pre:* the truth covers the image time
- *Post:* noise-free, every observation is the exact projection; the noise is the injected noise only
- *Test:* `tests/tools/ideal_tracks.cpp` “ideal tracks are projections of fixed landmarks…”
- *Test:* `tests/tools/ideal_tracks.cpp` “ideal tracks add the noise and only the noise…”
- *Deviates from the paper:* reads the raw ground truth today, not the spline (#613)

**run_filter** (G4, Run the backend under test)

- *Pre:* R and Q are exactly the injected noise when matched; every backend uses the same S6d gate line
- *Post:* converged runs have NIS within its χ² band
- *Test:* `tests/tools/pose_consistency.cpp` “Pose consistency: errors drawn from the covariance give an ANEES…”
- *Deviates from the paper:* the IMU is the real one, not synthesized from the truth, so matched R and Q are not the true noise (#613)

**score** (G5, Score the run)

- *Pre:* the estimate and the truth overlap in time
- *Post:* the gauge is anchored once; errors are taken in the filter's own convention
- *Test:* `tests/tools/pose_consistency.cpp` “Pose consistency: the gauge anchor absorbs yaw and position…”
- *Deviates from the paper:* ATE is scored against the raw ground truth (traj_eval --dataset), not the truth the filter was given (#613)

**verdict** (G6, Judge the grid)

- *Pre:* every run scored, or marked diverged or unavailable
- *Post:* an unavailable result cannot be recorded

## Data structures

| Edge | Kind | Contents | Type | Invariants (✓ has a validator) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GroundTruth** | input | the EuRoC state_groundtruth_estimate0 poses (Vicon or Leica, 200 Hz) | `tools/include/branes/tools/ideal_tracks.hpp` `IdealTracks` | ✓ timestamps strictly increasing |
| **NoiseModel** | input | the gyro and accelerometer noise densities and bias random walks (VioConfig's), the track pixel σ S, and the seed | `sdk/include/branes/sdk/vio_backend.hpp` `VioConfig` | ✗ the filter's Q and the synthesis use the same densities; matched R equals S |
| **CameraCalib** | input | EuRoC cam0 intrinsics, distortion and camera↔IMU extrinsic | `tools/include/branes/tools/euroc_cam0.hpp` `euroc_cam0` | ✓ R_imu_cam ∈ SO(3) |
| **TruthSpline** | data | a uniform cumulative cubic B-spline on SO(3) × ℝ³ with control poses at ground-truth samples; pose, angular velocity and acceleration at any time inside its span | — | ✗ C² in position and rotation; ω and a match finite differences of its pose; ✗ stays within a stated bound of the ground truth (it approximates, it is not fitted) |
| **ImuStream** | data | 200 Hz samples ω_m = ω + b_g + n_g, a_m = Rᵀ(p̈ − g) + b_a + n_a, with b_g, b_a random walks | — | ✗ noise-free, integrated by the shipped S2, it reproduces the spline's pose; ✗ the noise matches NoiseModel's densities (per-axis variance, per-sample whiteness) |
| **TrackStream** | data | per image time, pixel observations of fixed landmarks seen from the truth's camera pose, plus N(0, S²) per axis | `tools/include/branes/tools/ideal_tracks.hpp` `IdealTracks` | ✗ noise-free, each observation is the exact projection of its landmark; ✗ the added noise is N(0, S²) per axis and nothing else |
| **Estimate** | data | the backend's trajectory (TUM) and per-frame pose covariance in its own error convention | `tools/src/vio_pipeline.cpp` `write_tum` | ✗ finite; one pose per tracked frame |
| **Score** | data | window 4-DoF ATE ÷ OpenVINS mono, the pose NEES split, the world-yaw σ over the run, NIS | `tools/include/branes/tools/pose_consistency.hpp` `PoseConsistencySummary` | ✗ scored against TruthSpline, not the raw ground truth; ✗ a run with no trajectory counts as diverged; one that cannot be scored is unavailable |
| **GateVerdict** | output | PASS, FAIL or UNAVAILABLE per condition, and the recorded results | `scripts/euroc-consistency.json` | — |

- **NoiseModel** precision: double throughout; the bench is not a precision study

## Schedule

The driver is `scripts/euroc-consistency.sh`.

```text
on run (sequence × S × seed × R):
  fit_spline
  synth_imu
  synth_tracks
  run_filter
  score
on grid complete:
  verdict
```

## System invariants

| Invariant | Check | Status |
|---|---|---|
| converged runs with matched R have normalized NIS ≈ 1 (the noise model is true) | — | failing — 1.65–4.3 with the real IMU and the raw ground truth (#613) |
| the synthesized IMU, noise-free, integrates back to the truth | — | unknown |
| every backend runs with the same S6d gate line | — | holds — since #608 both use the χ² quantile; before it the R-IEKF used 16 per dof |
| each condition's result changes with the estimator, not with the gate's line | — | failing — conditions 1 and 2 flip with the gate line (#613) |
