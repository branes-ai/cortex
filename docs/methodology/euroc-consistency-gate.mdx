For each sequence, track-noise level S, seed and R (matched to S, or the filter's default): fit a smooth truth through the ground truth, synthesize the IMU and the feature tracks from it with known noise, run the backend under test, score its trajectory and pose consistency against that truth; then judge the grid. Every backend runs with the same S6d gate.

## Sources

- **kim1995** — M.-J. Kim, M.-S. Kim and S. Y. Shin, "A General Construction Scheme for Unit Quaternion Curves with Simple High Order Derivatives", SIGGRAPH 1995 (the cumulative B-spline on rotations).
- **sommer2020** — C. Sommer, V. Usenko, D. Schubert, N. Demmel and D. Cremers, "Efficient Derivative Computation for Cumulative B-Splines on Lie Groups", CVPR 2020 (angular velocity and acceleration of a cumulative spline).
- **mueggler2018** — E. Mueggler, G. Gallego, H. Rebecq and D. Scaramuzza, "Continuous-Time Visual-Inertial Odometry for Event Cameras", IEEE T-RO 34(6), 2018 (an IMU synthesized from a spline trajectory).
- **barshalom2001** — Y. Bar-Shalom, X. R. Li and T. Kirubarajan, "Estimation with Applications to Tracking and Navigation", Wiley 2001 (NEES, NIS and their χ² bands).
- **zhang2018** — Z. Zhang and D. Scaramuzza, "A Tutorial on Quantitative Trajectory Evaluation for Visual(-Inertial) Odometry", IROS 2018 (4-DoF alignment, ATE, RPE).

## Transformations

Each has the shape *(state, inputs, params) → (state′, diagnostics)*.

| Stage | Node | Does | Reads → writes | Paper | Status | Code | Bench |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **fit_spline** | ground-truth poses → a cumulative cubic B-spline through control poses sampled from them | GroundTruth → TruthSpline | kim1995, the cumulative basis; derivatives from sommer2020 | implemented | `tools/include/branes/tools/gt_spline.hpp` | — |
| G2 | **synth_imu** | the spline at the real IMU timestamps → ω and specific force in the body frame, plus noise and bias walks at the configured densities | TruthSpline, NoiseModel → ImuStream | mueggler2018, the IMU from a spline trajectory | implemented | `tools/include/branes/tools/gt_spline.hpp` | — |
| G3 | **synth_tracks** | the camera pose from the truth at each image time → projections of seeded landmarks, plus N(0, S²) | TruthSpline, CameraCalib, NoiseModel → TrackStream | zhang2018, ideal measurements for a controlled evaluation | implemented | `tools/include/branes/tools/ideal_tracks.hpp` | — |
| G4 | **run_filter** | the IMU and the tracks → the estimate, with R matched to S or the default, Q from NoiseModel, and the shipped χ² gate for every backend | ImuStream, TrackStream, NoiseModel → Estimate | barshalom2001, a filter told the true noise is consistent | implemented | `tools/src/vio_pipeline.cpp` | — |
| G5 | **score** | the estimate against the truth → window ATE ratio, pose NEES split, world-yaw σ, NIS | Estimate, TruthSpline → Score | zhang2018, 4-DoF alignment and ATE | implemented | `tools/include/branes/tools/pose_consistency.hpp` | — |
| G6 | **verdict** | every run's score → the three conditions (no divergence, matched beats default, no yaw collapse) and the gate's verdict | Score → GateVerdict | barshalom2001, consistency tests over Monte-Carlo runs | implemented | `scripts/euroc-consistency.sh` | — |

### Contracts

**fit_spline** (G1, Fit the truth)

- *Pre:* the ground truth covers the sequence; no gap longer than one knot interval
- *Post:* pose, ω and a are defined on the span; ω and a agree with finite differences of the pose; it stays within a bound of the ground truth
- *Test:* `tests/tools/gt_spline.cpp` “gt spline: velocity, acceleration and body rate are the derivatives of its pose…”
- *Test:* `tests/tools/gt_spline.cpp` “gt spline: it stays close to the ground truth it was built from…”

**synth_imu** (G2, Synthesize the IMU)

- *Pre:* the IMU timestamps lie inside the spline's span
- *Post:* noise-free and integrated by the shipped S2 from both samples of each interval, it closes on the spline within a tenth of 0.5 px over a clone window; the noise statistics match the densities
- *Test:* `tests/tools/gt_spline.cpp` “gt spline: S2 closure, the shipped S2 within what the camera resolves (#616)…”
- *Test:* `tests/tools/gt_spline.cpp` “gt spline: the synthesized noise has the configured densities…”

**synth_tracks** (G3, Synthesize the tracks)

- *Pre:* the truth covers the image time
- *Post:* noise-free, every observation is the exact projection; the noise is the injected noise only
- *Test:* `tests/tools/ideal_tracks.cpp` “ideal tracks are projections of fixed landmarks…”
- *Test:* `tests/tools/ideal_tracks.cpp` “ideal tracks add the noise and only the noise…”
- *Test:* `tests/tools/ideal_tracks.cpp` “ideal tracks can draw the noise in normalized coordinates…”

**run_filter** (G4, Run the backend under test)

- *Pre:* R and Q are exactly the injected noise when matched; every backend uses the same S6d gate line
- *Post:* converged runs have NIS within its χ² band
- *Test:* `tests/tools/pose_consistency.cpp` “Pose consistency: errors drawn from the covariance give an ANEES…”

**score** (G5, Score the run)

- *Pre:* the estimate and the truth overlap in time
- *Post:* the gauge is anchored once; errors are taken in the filter's own convention
- *Test:* `tests/tools/pose_consistency.cpp` “Pose consistency: the gauge anchor absorbs yaw and position…”

**verdict** (G6, Judge the grid)

- *Pre:* every run scored, or marked diverged or unavailable
- *Post:* an unavailable result cannot be recorded

## Data structures

| Edge | Kind | Contents | Type | Invariants (✓ has a validator) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GroundTruth** | input | the EuRoC state_groundtruth_estimate0 poses (Vicon or Leica, 200 Hz) | `tools/include/branes/tools/ideal_tracks.hpp` `IdealTracks` | ✓ timestamps strictly increasing |
| **NoiseModel** | input | the gyro and accelerometer noise densities and bias random walks (VioConfig's), the track pixel σ S, and the seed | `sdk/include/branes/sdk/vio_backend.hpp` `VioConfig` | ✗ the filter's Q and the synthesis use the same densities; matched R equals S |
| **CameraCalib** | input | EuRoC cam0 intrinsics, distortion and camera↔IMU extrinsic | `tools/include/branes/tools/euroc_cam0.hpp` `euroc_cam0` | ✓ R_imu_cam ∈ SO(3) |
| **TruthSpline** | data | a uniform cumulative cubic B-spline on SO(3) × ℝ³ with control poses at ground-truth samples every 0.05 s; pose, angular velocity and acceleration at any time inside its span | `tools/include/branes/tools/gt_spline.hpp` `PoseSpline` | ✓ C² in position and rotation; ω and a match finite differences of its pose; ✓ stays within a stated bound of the ground truth (it approximates, it is not fitted) |
| **ImuStream** | data | at the real IMU timestamps, ω_m = ω + b_g + n_g, a_m = Rᵀ(p̈ − g) + b_a + n_a, with b_g, b_a random walks from zero | `tools/include/branes/tools/gt_spline.hpp` `synthesize_imu` | ✓ noise-free, integrated by the shipped S2 from both samples of each interval, it closes on the spline within a tenth of 0.5 px over a clone window, in rotation and position (see s2_closure, #616); ✓ the noise matches NoiseModel's densities (per-axis variance, per-sample whiteness) |
| **TrackStream** | data | per image time, pixel observations of fixed landmarks seen from the truth's camera pose, plus N(0, S²) per axis | `tools/include/branes/tools/ideal_tracks.hpp` `IdealTracks` | ✓ noise-free, each observation is the exact projection of its landmark; ✓ the added noise is N(0, (S/fx)²) per axis in normalized coordinates and nothing else |
| **Estimate** | data | the backend's trajectory (TUM) and per-frame pose covariance in its own error convention | `tools/src/vio_pipeline.cpp` `write_tum` | ✗ finite; one pose per tracked frame |
| **Score** | data | window 4-DoF ATE ÷ OpenVINS mono, the pose NEES split, the world-yaw σ over the run, NIS | `tools/include/branes/tools/pose_consistency.hpp` `PoseConsistencySummary` | ✗ scored against TruthSpline, not the raw ground truth; ✗ a run with no trajectory counts as diverged; one that cannot be scored is unavailable |
| **GateVerdict** | output | PASS, FAIL or UNAVAILABLE per condition, and the recorded results | `scripts/euroc-consistency.json` | — |

- **NoiseModel** precision: double throughout; the bench is not a precision study

## Schedule

The driver is `scripts/euroc-consistency.sh`.

```text
on run (sequence × S × seed × R):
  fit_spline
  synth_imu
  synth_tracks
  run_filter
  score
on grid complete:
  verdict
```
