This section tells how cortex got its visual-inertial odometry working: every defect,
investigation and fix along the way, as one story. It is written for an engineer who has to
**build a VIO pipeline from first principles, or certify a new one**. Most of what went wrong
here goes wrong in any MSCKF-style pipeline, and a unit-test suite that passes doesn't catch it.

## The main lesson

Over about six months, **three results that looked solid turned out to be artifacts of the
test setup**: a compiler "bug" that was an unsequenced random draw, a filter "inconsistency" that
was a velocity step in the synthetic world, and a "consistent" control that fed the filter exact
sensors. Each time, the filter was blamed first. The storyline states every revision next to the
claim it revises, because how a wrong conclusion was reached is as useful as the right one.

The other lessons, in one line each:

- **Synthetic unit tests are necessary and nowhere near sufficient.** When the VIO epic closed,
  every part was unit-tested and nothing had run on real data. The first real run was off by 26 km.
- **ATE hides over-confidence.** A filter can be accurate to 30 cm with a covariance a hundred
  times too small. Report NEES and NIS beside ATE, always.
- **Measure before fixing; ship candidates default-off.** Several textbook fixes made things worse
  once they were measured end to end.

## Vocabulary

The stages follow the [canonical pipeline](../pipeline/canonical-pipeline.md), whose contracts are
described one page per stage in the [VIO Pipeline](../pipeline/overview.mdx) section.

| Stage | Role | Stage | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| S0 | [sensor models](../pipeline/stages/s0-sensor-models.mdx) | S6a | Jacobians |
| S1 | [initialization](../pipeline/stages/s1-initialization.mdx) | S6b | null-space projection |
| S2 | [IMU propagation](../pipeline/stages/s2-imu-propagation.mdx) | S6c | compression |
| S3 | [clone augmentation](../pipeline/stages/s3-augmentation.mdx) | S6d | gating |

| S9 | [marginalization](../pipeline/stages/s9-marginalization.mdx) | S10 | [online calibration](../pipeline/stages/s10-online-calibration.mdx) |

There are two backends:

- **Body-frame:** `MsckfBackend`, an MSCKF whose error state is expressed in the body frame.
- **R-IEKF:** `MsckfInvariantBackend` / `InvariantVioBackend`, a right-invariant EKF on SE₂(3).

The metrics (ATE, RPE, NEES, NIS) are defined on [Reading the Metrics](../pipeline/metrics.mdx).

## Where it stands (2026-11-01)

| Area | State |
|---|---|
| Body-frame filter | Accurate on EuRoC (ATE 0.27–0.76 m) but over-confident there (NEES/dof 43–436) and on the synthetic world under matched noise (median pose NEES 26 against 6). The mechanism is the yaw gauge leak in S2/S3; its share of the EuRoC NEES isn't attributed yet. It stays the EuRoC accuracy reference. |
| R-IEKF | Consistent on the synthetic world under noise: normalized full NEES 0.55–1.84 over 21 seeds at the default onset, and 0.39–1.88 over 21 seeds with a slow 2 s start. **Less accurate on EuRoC** than the body-frame filter (V1_01 1.31 m against 0.29 m), and its EuRoC NEES verdict is open. Closing that gap is the central open problem. |
| Initialization | Robust with static init. Dynamic init is limited by excitation and has open SfM defects. |
| Real time | Real-time factor about 0.10×. |
